Frequently Asked Questions

Why must the first course of a retaining wall be buried?
The bottom course of blocks must be buried in a compacted gravel leveling pad (at least 6 inches deep, plus 1 inch buried per foot of wall height). This prevents the toe of the wall from sliding forward under lateral soil pressure.
When is geogrid reinforcement legally required on a retaining wall?
Under International Residential Code (IRC) and National Concrete Masonry Association (NCMA) standards, gravity retaining walls over 3 to 4 feet tall require structural geogrid fabric extending horizontally back into the compacted soil every two courses.
Why is clean crushed gravel required behind retaining walls instead of dirt?
Water trapped behind a wall is the #1 cause of wall failure (hydrostatic pressure). Clean 3/4" crushed stone (#57 gravel) allows groundwater to drop instantly to the bottom perforated pipe, preventing the wall from leaning or toppling.
